Our story

A Chinese-founded dessert studio, made in Sheffield.

PhDessert began as a small-batch kitchen shaped by home flavours, pastry craft and a researcher’s attention to detail. The menu moves naturally between Chinese pastries, pâtisserie and contemporary cakes.

The PhD reference stays quietly in the name and the tiny graduation cap. Everything else is about flavour, texture and the pleasure of something made by hand.
